Detailed Description

template<typename Vector_>
class FEAT::Solver::CGS2Orthogonalizer< Vector_ >

Classical Gram-Schmidt with a (conditional) reorthogonalization sweep (CGS2)

This extends the batched classical Gram-Schmidt procedure of ClassicGramSchmidtOrthogonalizer by a corrective second orthogonalization sweep, recovering a numerical stability close to modified Gram-Schmidt while keeping the dot products batched (at most two reductions per step instead of one reduction per basis vector).

The corrective sweep is either always performed (see set_force_reortho()) or triggered automatically by the classic DGKS "twice is enough" criterion: the sweep is redone whenever the orthogonalized vector lost too much of its length, i.e. \(\|v_\text{new}\| < \tau \|w\|\) (equivalently \(\text{nrm\_sqr} < \tau^2 \langle w,w\rangle\)). With the DGKS threshold the algebraic norm is only trusted when the vector kept a healthy fraction of its length, and any heavier cancellation pays exactly one corrective reduction.

Computes the batched dot products of target against the leading basis vectors.

A single batched reduction fills dots_out[0..num_basis) with \(\langle \text{target}, v_k\rangle\) and returns the self-dot \(\langle \text{target}, \text{target}\rangle\) (basis slot num_basis) in self_dot.

Parameters
[in]targetThe vector all dot products are computed with.
[in]num_basisThe number of leading basis vectors to project against.
[out]dot_outA host array of size num_basis receiving the inner dot products.
[out]self_dotReceives the self-dot of target.
