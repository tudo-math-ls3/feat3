Sets up a persistent cuSPARSE SpMV/BSRMV workspace for this matrix.

For 1x1 blocks this defers to MatVecMultCSRDense::init_workspace_cuda(). For square blocks with 32-bit indices, this creates the (persistent) cuSPARSE matrix descriptor used by the bsrmv path once, so that consecutive apply() calls do not have to recreate it. For all other cases (non-square blocks, or 64-bit indices) no workspace is required and this is a no-op.

Warning
This assumes the row-pointer and column-index arrays (and their device pointers) do not change after this call.
