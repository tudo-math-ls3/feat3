Sets up a persistent cuSPARSE SpMV workspace for this matrix.

Creates the (persistent) cuSPARSE CSR matrix descriptor and the SpMV buffer once, so that consecutive apply() calls do not have to recreate them. The returned workspace is stored in workspace (a type-erased Util::CuSparseSPMVWorkSpace behind a shared_ptr).

Warning
This assumes the row-pointer and column-index arrays (and their device pointers) do not change after this call.
